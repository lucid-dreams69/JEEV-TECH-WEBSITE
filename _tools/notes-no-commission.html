Why Servizo takes no commission

DecisionBusiness model1 min read

Read the note

The default model for a service platform is a percentage of every transaction. It is simple to explain, it scales with usage, and it is what investors expect to see.

We asked a narrower question: what is the platform actually being paid for? The answer is the software — verification, scheduling, documentation, the record a professional builds. It is not the work itself, which the professional does. Charging a share of that work would be charging for something we did not do, and it would make our revenue rise as the professional's margin falls.

So Servizo charges a subscription for the tools and takes nothing from the job. Customers pay no platform fee today; if one becomes necessary to run the infrastructure, it will be introduced for that reason and stated plainly. We are paid for the software rather than a share of the work: what we earn tracks how useful the tools are, not how much the professional was paid for the job.
