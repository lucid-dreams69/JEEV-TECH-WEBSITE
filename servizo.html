Definition

What Servizo is

Digital infrastructure for the real-world service economy, designed so that trust is built into the system rather than assumed. Servizo includes a marketplace: customers find and book professionals, and professionals find work. That is a capability, not the product.

The product is what happens after the booking. Identity and capability are verified before anyone is visible to a customer. The job is scheduled, carried out, documented, paid for, and its completion verified. All of it is written into a record that belongs to the professional. Finding work is the beginning of the system, not the end of it.

Most platforms in this industry carry a transaction and stop when it closes. Servizo is built as a system of record for professional work, so that a finished job leaves behind verifiable history instead of nothing at all — and so the power stays with the people doing the work rather than with the platform standing between them and their customers.

Problem

What is actually broken

Hiring someone to do work in your home still runs on guesswork. Availability is a phone call, the price arrives after the fact, and a professional's track record lives in other people's memories.

The professional has the mirror image of the same problem. Work arrives unpredictably, reputation does not travel, and years of good work leave behind no record that can be shown to the next customer.

Audience

Who it helps

Service professionals — the people whose work happens in person, in real time, in other people's homes and businesses. Electricians, plumbers, cleaning professionals, home repair specialists. People whose skills are essential and whose industry has been underserved by technology that was built for other models.

And the businesses they become. A professional working alone and a firm running a team of them need the same record of the work, so both are served by the same application: at Tier 2, Servizo Pro opens into Business Mode, which adds employees, recruitment, branches, workforce management and analytics. It is a mode inside the professional's app, not a separate product to graduate onto.

Products

Two apps, one system

Servizo

For customers. It aims to make hiring a service predictable and trustworthy — from finding the right professional to reviewing completed work.

Servizo Pro

For service professionals. Less a source of leads than a working system: verified identity, scheduling, documentation, and payment in one place. Every finished job adds to a record the professional owns — so the app is also a portfolio, a verifiable work history for people whose work has never left one behind.

Servizo Pro comes in three tiers, and the first of them is free.

Starter
Free. A portfolio the professional keeps, campaigns to advertise their own work, and an account that can be hired by other providers on the platform.
Professional
Subscription. The full working system: verified identity, scheduling, the job itself, payment, and the record it leaves behind.
Business Mode
Tier 2, for a provider who now employs people. Adds recruitment, employees, branches, workforce management and analytics, all reading from the same record.

Built

What exists today

Both applications are built and work end to end. Anyone can register and use the free Starter tier. Verification is what makes a professional bookable by customers, and identity is not confirmed by a document someone sends us: it is checked against an independent authority, and we keep only what is needed to show the check was made. A customer can find a verified professional, book a job, and follow it through scheduling to completion. Payment is handled in the app.

A job is not finished because one side says so. Completion is confirmed by the customer and the professional between them, recorded in the app on both sides, and kept by Servizo as the record of what happened.

It is not live. Nothing above describes users we have or work we have processed — it describes software that is finished and waiting to launch.

Business model

How Servizo earns, and what we won't do for it

Servizo takes no commission. A professional keeps what they earn. The platform is funded by a subscription they pay for the tools and reach it provides, priced openly, so that what we are paid for is the software rather than a share of someone else's work.

Customers pay no platform fee today. If one becomes necessary to run the infrastructure, it will be introduced for that reason and stated plainly when it is.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers. We use what running the work requires — verification, scheduling, payment, the record — and we analyse aggregated, anonymised system data to improve reliability, fraud detection, search quality and the product itself.

A company that says people are not products cannot fund itself by selling them.

Rationale

Why start here

The service economy forces engineering decisions to survive real-world constraints: identity, scheduling, payments, documentation, disputes, and accountability. It is an environment where systems either work or fail in public.

That is the right place to learn to build. It also has to pay for itself — the longer-horizon work depends on it — and a company that believes in life-centric technology should demonstrate that on something useful to people doing real work before reaching for the larger ambition.
