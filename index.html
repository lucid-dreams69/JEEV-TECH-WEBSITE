About JEEV TECH

JEEV TECH is an engineering company, built on one rule: whoever uses a system keeps control of it. Most technology is built the other way round. That one rule decides what we build, what we research, and what we refuse.

Builds
Digital infrastructure for the real-world service economy. Servizo is the first product.
Studies
Long-horizon research in intelligence, health, energy, and large-scale infrastructure.
Refuses
Anything irreversible, and anything that removes a person's ability to understand, control, or change their own decisions.
Stage
Incorporated in India, pre-launch. The first product is built and launching soon.

Current product

Servizo

Infrastructure for service work — the jobs that happen in person, in someone's home or business. It takes no commission: the professional keeps what they earn, and we are paid a subscription for the software instead. The record of the work belongs to them, not to us. Built and launching soon.

The one line

We design systems that preserve human agency, accountability, and the ability to change course.

Which gives us one line we will not cross: we do not build the irreversible, and we do not build permanent control. Most tradeoffs are weighable, because most things can be undone. That one is not, at any price.
