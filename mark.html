The identity

The Mark of JEEV

Life is not static. It transforms, adapts, and emerges.

Examine the object

00 — The form

One continuous surface

The JEEV mark is a single ribbon, folded in three dimensions until it supports itself. Nothing is joined to it and nothing hangs from it: every part of the form is the same surface, seen at a different point in its turn.

01 — Continuity

No beginning. No end

The form is drawn from the Möbius band — a surface with no conventional start and no conventional finish. Follow the ribbon from any point and it returns to that point, reoriented but unbroken. That is the mark's first principle: transformation does not require an ending.

02 — Emergence

New, from what exists

At the upper right, the surface climbs out of its own loop. Nothing is added at that point — the ribbon that rises is the ribbon that circulates. The second principle: the new is generated from the existing, not attached to it.

03 — Life

The strands inside

Within the folds run parallel strands, invisible at a distance. They refer to the way life carries what it knows: structure holding information, passed forward, transformed. The mark does not depict a molecule. It borrows the principle.

04 — Technology

Drawn from life. Built by engineering

Seen whole again, the mark is neither organic nor mechanical. The geometry comes from life; the execution — one surface, resolved until it could be machined — comes from engineering. JEEV works in the space between the two.
