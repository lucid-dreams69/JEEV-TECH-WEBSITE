Founder’s note

Why the company exists

JEEV TECH did not begin with a business plan. It began with a question: what kind of world are we building, and who is technology actually serving?

Technology has advanced at extraordinary speed. Human wellbeing has not kept pace. Systems get built to maximize engagement, encourage dependency and collect personal data, and they work — that is the part that concerns me. The cost lands on privacy, on trust, and on the relationship between people and the tools they use.

I stopped asking what product I should build, and started asking what technology should exist to accomplish.

I work from first principles. Before accepting that a system has to work the way it does, I want to know why it exists and whether it could be rebuilt from its foundations. I read outside engineering for the same reason. The useful answer is rarely contained in one discipline.

I do not want to build technology because it is possible. If JEEV TECH is remembered, I hope it is for the principles we refused to compromise rather than the products we shipped.

Sanjeev R V

Hiring

Who belongs here

The single quality we look for is curiosity: people who ask why a system works the way it does, and keep asking when no one is checking. Credentials and experience matter, but they are not the filter.

That is a requirement of the work rather than a preference. We value people who question assumptions, think from first principles, and improve ideas rather than simply executing them.
